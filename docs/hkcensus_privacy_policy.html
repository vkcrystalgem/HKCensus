HK Census Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
Developer: VK Crystal (vkcrystalgem)
Android package: com.vkcrystalgem.hkcensus

This policy explains how HK Census handles information when you use the Android app, visit this privacy-policy page, or contact the developer.

HK Census displays public Hong Kong statistical data. The installed app does not require an account, collect personal information, display advertising, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information collection and use

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or other personal information. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, personal photos, or advertising identifier.

Chart selections and touch interactions are used in memory to display the charts. They are not recorded as a usage history or sent to the developer. The app does not operate a developer-hosted user database and does not sell or share personal information collected through the app.

Public statistics and local app files

The charts use public statistics from the Hong Kong Census and Statistics Department. These are aggregate statistics, not information collected from people using this app. The statistical data is bundled with the app and can be viewed offline.

App files, bundled statistics, and files managed by Android or the app framework may be stored on your device. You can remove locally stored app data through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Device backups, if enabled, are managed under your Android or Google backup settings.

Advertising, diagnostics, and platform services

HK Census does not include AdMob, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or an additional developer-operated crash-reporting integration.

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, device, usage, and diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings. For example, when you opt in to sharing usage and diagnostics, Google may provide app performance and crash reports to the developer through Play Console. These platform services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

If you choose a source link in the app, your browser opens the Census and Statistics Department website. Opening this policy also uses your browser. The browser and destination website may process information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The developer does not control those websites' data practices.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies added by the developer. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages websites. GitHub's handling of website visits and support interactions is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Support information, retention, and deletion

If you submit a GitHub issue, your GitHub account name and the information you voluntarily include are used to respond to and resolve your enquiry. GitHub issues are public and may remain available after they are closed. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in an issue.

The app has no user accounts or developer-held personal-data records to delete. For information you post to GitHub, use GitHub's editing or deletion controls where available. You can also request removal of information from a repository issue through the contact method below, without repeating the information in public. GitHub's own retention and deletion practices are governed by its privacy statement.

Security

The app does not transmit personal information to a developer-operated server. This policy page and the external services linked here use HTTPS. Keep your device and GitHub account secure, and avoid posting sensitive information in public support requests.

Children's privacy

The app does not request or collect personal information from users, including children. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact the developer using the method below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. Updated versions will be posted at this URL with a revised last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a privacy enquiry on the HK Census GitHub issue page. A GitHub account may be required to submit an issue. Please keep your enquiry free of personal or sensitive information.
